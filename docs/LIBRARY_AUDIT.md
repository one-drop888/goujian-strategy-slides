# Presentation Library quality audit

**Historical V1.0 audit below.** V1.1 adds 8 semantic routes and implements image-insight through 4 reusable multimodal compositions: 86 entries (74 implemented / 6 partial / 6 designed), 27 compositions, 15 profiles and 12 scenarios. All 15 profiles were rendered with a common image/text payload; they remain related variants, not independent design systems. Current evidence: [V1.1 validation](V1_1_VALIDATION.md).

Review date: 2026-10-14. Counts were read from the registries and checked against the actual entries.

## Storylines: 28 distinct sequences, related management families

No two entries have identical recommended sequences. Reviews intentionally share result/gap/cause/action logic: monthly review emphasizes short-cycle change; quarterly review adds resource/structural judgment; annual review adds durable learning and future direction. These are useful variants, not entirely independent reasoning theories.

Research/VOC explicitly separates method, frequency, segment differences and interpretation. Technical/AI proposals emphasize architecture, controls, risk and staged adoption. Cost decisions move from cost concentration to alternatives and allocation. Training establishes understanding, examples and application. These are materially different management tasks.

Weakness: selection is heuristic and several trigger phrases overlap. The registry does not prove a particular source supports its recommended narrative. Agents must justify selection and adapt page order; no automatic causal inference is claimed.

## Archetypes: vocabulary versus rendering capability

78 catalog entries resolve to 23 recipes and 21 rendering functions. Status after audit: **65 IMPLEMENTED / 6 PARTIAL / 7 DESIGNED**.

Reusable aliases are deliberate where the information structure matches: comparison/segmentation/regional-performance share comparable columns; driver-tree/root-cause/organization share a bounded hierarchy; decision/recommendation/key-message share a focal statement. These are different questions using shared geometry, not different rendering implementations.

- **DESIGNED, rejected at build:** combo, scatter, bubble, waterfall, bridge, heatmap, image-insight. The candidate had misleading ordinary-chart or generic-table mappings; those are no longer treated as support.
- **PARTIAL, disclosed:** map and relationship-map show component groups; sankey-flow has no weighted flow encoding; fishbone is a cause tree; histogram requires pre-binned values; case-study supports quote/interpretation rather than a complete case schema.
- `research-method` now retains sample/method/period notes and limitations rather than putting them into an empty generic scorecard.

Implemented means an appropriate bounded composition exists. It does not mean every content length, chart subtype or complex organizational structure fits.

## Compositions and primitives

23 recipe identifiers share 21 functions. `compare-2` adds a cardinality constraint to comparison; chart-full/chart-with-insight use the same chart primitive with optional interpretation. They are variants, not a declarative layout engine. A 23-slide fixture gallery exercises every recipe; native chart workbooks and absence of flattened slide images are checked.

Primitives are native PowerPoint text, shapes, lines, charts and tables. The data renderer was separated from structural slides. Title, source, page number and reusable text/rule helpers are shared. Some older diagrams still use explicit sizes; no speculative renderer framework was introduced.

## Profiles: 15 related presets with observable differences

All 15 differ beyond accent color in resolved typography, margin, title rule, table header/banding, metric treatment, chart grid and cover treatment. A three-slide shared probe (section, research-method table, indexed trend) was built and rendered for each. Dark-chart legend contrast was corrected after that comparison.

The presets form related families: dense analysis (consulting/finance/executive-dense), general management (executive-clean/corporate/strategy/sales), evidence/technical (research/product-tech), and spacious communication (minimal/luxury/launch/marketing/training), plus dark-executive. Some differences are intentionally subtle. Fifteen names do not imply fifteen bespoke visual systems.

Unused claims for image treatment, gradients and automatic fallback fonts were removed. V1 has no native image-insight renderer. A font-family change requires fonts to be installed on both authoring and recipient systems.

## Recipes and benchmarks

15 recipes choose storyline, profile and suggested slide-count range; they are not imported PowerPoint templates. Ten scenarios produce **60 slides** and include analytical, comparison, research, framework, summary and action pages. The gallery adds coverage for section, timeline and pyramid compositions missing from the primary scenarios.

The ten examples are short synthetic scenarios. They prove the bounded workflow across multiple structures, not suitability for every board deck or every domain. Executive density still depends on meaningful evidence supplied by the agent; adding more text is not the remedy for weak content.
